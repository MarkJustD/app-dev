# My Current Favorite Series: 
Dead-End Job (2026)
 
## About
A debt-stricken guy takes shady part-time gigs from a mysterious staffing agency for 50x the pay, 
only to realize the workplace is literally trying to kill him.
 
### Why I like it
- Funny jokes about normal jobs, but turned into crazy and scary survival games.
- Fast and exciting story where every single work day feels like a ticking bomb.
- Great surprises that change who is really in control between the workers and the bosses.
 
> "Because there's no stronger Yang energy than the struggle to make a living." — Mr. S
